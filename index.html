Why this matters

The hat isn't old. The box we keep it in is.

For a lot of us, the conical hat belongs in one place: a history lesson, a photo shoot, a rice field. So pairing it with a hoodie feels a little odd, like someone might judge. But the gap between “heritage” and “daily life” has real consequences.

01

Demand has dropped

VOV World reported that modern demand for conical hats has fallen, making it harder for craft villages like Trí Lê to keep the craft going.

Behind the hat

Every hat comes from a village.

Trí Lê

Trí Lê is a village known for making traditional palm-leaf hats. VOV World featured it and reported that falling modern demand is a challenge for keeping the craft alive.

Chuồng village

In Chuồng village, artisan Tạ Thu Hương says the traditional techniques stay while the designs keep evolving, so the hat can also be décor, a gift, a performance prop or a tourism item.

Sources
  1. VOV World, "Tri Le – a village that makes traditional palm-leaf hats", Aug 1, 2016
